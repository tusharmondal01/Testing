Step 04 · Export

Export to Premiere Pro.

Upload the SRT and download the XML. The subtitle timings decide when each image starts. Images butt up against each other, so the timeline has no gaps.

In Premiere Pro: File, Import, pick the .xml. If you left the folder blank, Premiere will ask you to locate 001.jpg once and then finds the rest.
